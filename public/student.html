Before you begin

After you have done the reading, you will talk it through with an AI tutor. This takes the place of a reading quiz. There are no trick questions. The goal is to explain what you understood in your own words, and it is fine to say what confused you.

What you should know
  • The tutor is an AI, set up by your instructor to reflect their teaching style. It is not your instructor, and it can make mistakes.
  • This conversation is recorded. Your instructor will review it, along with an AI-drafted summary of what you explained.
  • What is collected: your name, the messages you write, the tutor's replies, and the times they were sent. Nothing else.
  • Your messages are sent to an AI service (Anthropic) to produce the tutor's replies. Your name is not sent to it. Please do not include private personal information in your messages.
  • Only you (on this device) and your instructor can see this conversation. This page uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking.
